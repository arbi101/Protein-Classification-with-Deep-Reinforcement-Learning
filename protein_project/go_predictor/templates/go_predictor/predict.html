How does it work?

  • Predict GO: Enter a protein sequence in FASTA format and click on "Predict GO" to obtain functional predictions of the protein based on Gene Ontology terms.
  • Generate 2D Structure: Click on "Generate 2D Structure" to visualize a 2D representation of the protein using optimization algorithms (Hill Climbing, Simulated Annealing, Monte Carlo, Replica Exchange MC, or Q-Learning). Choose your algorithm and configure parameters for optimal results.
  • Generate 3D Structure (AlphaFold): Click on "Generate 3D Structure (AlphaFold)" to predict the complete 3D structure of your protein. The tool first searches the AlphaFold database, and if unavailable, uses ESMFold for real-time prediction.
