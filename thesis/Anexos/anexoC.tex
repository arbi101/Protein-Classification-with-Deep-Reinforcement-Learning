\section*{Appendix C - \tituloanexoC}

This appendix presents a UML use case diagram derived from the functional requirements described in Chapter~\ref{cap3}. In UML, use cases are represented as ellipses inside the system boundary, while actors and external services are placed outside the boundary. Non-functional requirements are treated as quality constraints that support these behaviours rather than as separate use cases.
